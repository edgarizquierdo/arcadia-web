What it solves

From a complex territory to an understandable decision.

Atlas BioScan integrates information that is usually scattered across maps, official sources, time series and technical analyses. The platform brings it together, interprets it and turns it into a single view to answer three questions: what is happening, where action is needed and how to monitor it.

Analytical evidence

Evolution tells the full story.

Atlas goes beyond showing current conditions. Historical series and spatial condition help distinguish a temporary fluctuation from persistent deterioration and assess what happens after an intervention or wildfire.

Post-fire monitoring

Measure impact, evolution and recovery.

Historical NDVI and NDWI series contextualize the event, while post-fire monitoring records affected area, spectral recovery and completed actions.

Spatial condition

Detect persistence, extent and trend.

Multivariable analysis combines vigor, moisture and spatial anomaly to visualize how much territory deviates from its historical pattern and how it changes month by month without overwhelming the view with unnecessary information.
